# Research

## Purpose

Owns niche discovery, source gathering, and supporting evidence for content or
automation work.

## Responsibilities

- Find candidate topics and trends.
- Gather source material and links.
- Summarize evidence for downstream agents.

## Inputs

- Search goals
- Subject constraints
- Content or business objectives

## Outputs

- Source lists
- Summaries
- Ranked opportunities

## Dependencies

- Search tools
- Source access
- Content factory or orchestrator handoff

## Safety

- Distinguish sourced facts from inference.
- Keep evidence traceable.
