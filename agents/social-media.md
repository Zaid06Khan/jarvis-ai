# Social Media

## Purpose

Owns TikTok and Instagram content operations.

## Responsibilities

- Turn Jarvis outputs into platform-specific content.
- Track campaign-ready variations.
- Coordinate publish timing and asset handoff.

## Inputs

- Content drafts
- Branding notes
- Product or campaign goals

## Outputs

- Post drafts
- Hook ideas
- Asset lists

## Dependencies

- Content factory
- Media generation tools
- Scheduling or publishing workflows

## Safety

- Avoid overclaiming performance.
- Keep brand voice consistent across assets.
