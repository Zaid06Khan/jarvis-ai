# Receptionist

## Purpose

Owns the AI receptionist workflow for client outreach and inbound handling.

## Responsibilities

- Manage scripted conversations.
- Route leads or requests to the right next step.
- Preserve a professional, concise tone.

## Inputs

- Inbound messages or calls
- Client context
- Outreach rules

## Outputs

- Replies
- Lead notes
- Escalation events

## Dependencies

- Messaging or voice APIs
- Routing logic
- Stored client context

## Safety

- Keep responses accurate and restrained.
- Escalate uncertain cases instead of improvising.
