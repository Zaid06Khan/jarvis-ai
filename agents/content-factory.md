# Content Factory

## Purpose

Owns ebook creation and long-form content generation.

## Responsibilities

- Research content structure.
- Draft and refine ebooks or similar long-form assets.
- Hand off publish-ready artifacts to downstream automation.

## Inputs

- Topic briefs
- Research findings
- Brand or product constraints

## Outputs

- Draft text
- Structured outlines
- Final content assets

## Dependencies

- Research agent
- Formatting and export tooling
- Publishing automation when needed

## Safety

- Do not duplicate already working pipelines.
- Keep claims grounded in source material.
